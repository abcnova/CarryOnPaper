**CarryOnPaper 1.0.2** is a compatibility and safety update.

- Added configurable pickup support for all shulker box colors and anvils by default.
- New living mob types are allowed automatically unless excluded by the configured blacklist; player entities remain excluded.
- Added an optional Modrinth update notice for the console and server operators.
- Prevented pickup from deleting a container when its contents cannot be serialized.
- Preserved the existing Paper and Spigot product names.
